# An inland pond and a river reach, far from any sea. This is the case that used to have
# nothing holding fish in check: sharks and penguins only patrol the ocean, and hawks skim
# too few to matter, so a landlocked shoal grew until it hit the population cap.
# Two brakes are under test here:
#   1. Plankton — water now carries fertility that a shoal strips and that regrows slowly,
#      so an unfished pond levels off on its own and rich water breeds faster than thin water.
#   2. Otters — a sparse, territorial, semi-aquatic fish specialist that hunts in the water
#      and dens on the bank to breed, plus crocodiles working the river.
# Watch latest_nutrition.csv (fill % dropping as the shoal grows, recovering as it thins),
# latest_population.csv (fish should oscillate, not ramp), and latest_decisions.csv for
# Otter seek_breeding_ground roams.
