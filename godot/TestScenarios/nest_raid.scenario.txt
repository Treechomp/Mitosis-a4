# Faelings raiding a Sectid colony — the case the whole objective path exists for.
#
# Eight Faelings that kill individual Sectids are a rounding error: the colony hatches
# replacements faster than they can shoot. Eight that break NESTS end the colony. This scenario is
# the difference, and it exercises the two consequences a lost nest carries — the brood in its
# larvae slots dies with it (nest_destroyed), and losing the last nest of a colony ends that colony
# outright (colony_destroyed).
#
# It is also the two-way war: Sectids answer a siege with a siege (StructureRetaliationBias 8 turns
# their casual 0.5 aggression into 4.0 while their own nests are being broken), so the colony stops
# foraging and marches on the crystals. Which side wins is a genuine contest and it is NOT rigged
# in the raiders' favour — a first draft of this scenario gave the colony two nests and twelve
# Sectids, and the colony won decisively: all three crystals down by t=853 against a single nest
# lost. Twelve Sectids put out ~2.9 damage a tick against a crystal's 400; three Faelings put out
# ~1.65 against a nest's 200, and once their crystals fall there is nothing to respawn them. Elite
# raiders beat an OUTPOST, which is what this scenario is — six keepers against one nest and four
# workers.
#
# Watch latest_events.csv for structure_damaged by=Faeling, then nest_destroyed with a larvae_lost
# count, then colony_destroyed.

[world]
