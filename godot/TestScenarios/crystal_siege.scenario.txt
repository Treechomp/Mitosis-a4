# A Sectid colony besieging a Faeling crystal.
# Tests the objective path end to end: a structure is acquired without going through prey
# targeting, walked to, hit with the surface-to-surface reach, and destroyed — and the crystal's
# linked Faeling never comes back.
#
# The crystals are planted ON TOP OF the colony, and there is prey in the colony's midst. Both
# are load-bearing now.
#
# Sectid StructureAggression is 0.15 — a structure must be within 0.15x the distance of the prey
# the swarm would otherwise take — and with a HuntRange of 12 that is under two tiles, which no
# sane placement reaches. So opportunistic sieging is not the path here. RETALIATION is: the x8
# bias turns 0.15 into 1.2 while the colony's own nests are being broken, which at a 12-tile prey
# distance reaches about fourteen tiles, and the crystals sit inside six.
#
# The provocation is supplied by the crystals themselves. They hatch Faelings, the world census
# reads Sectids as the dominant faction (32 against 2), so the keepers besiege the NESTS — and the
# colony answers by breaking the crystals. That is the intended shape of the war rather than a
# scenario contrivance: nobody here attacks anything until they are attacked.
#
# THE PREY IS NOT SET DRESSING. A Sectid with no prey target does not siege at all
# (StructureIdleSeekRadius = 0): the aggression ratio measures a structure against the meal being
# passed up, and with no meal there is nothing to measure. Remove the rabbits and this scenario
# resolves the other way — the keepers take both nests and the crystals are never touched, which
# is exactly what it did before they were added.
#
#
# Crystals are also 1,600 HP now, not 400 — a crystal is the most permanent thing in the game and
# every one lost shrinks a twelve-member faction forever. Hence the longer budget.
#
# MEASURED. First crystal down at t=631 (`kind=Crystal;by=Sectid`), and the keepers take a nest
# back at t=4483. The SECOND crystal survives the 8,000-tick budget, which is why this scenario
# asserts "at least one Crystal destroyed" rather than "all": under the provoked-only rule a
# Sectid siege is episodic, and once the colony stops being attacked it goes back to hunting.
# Before sieging became provoked-only it took both crystals by t=2,408.
#
# Watch latest_events.csv for structure_damaged -> structure_destroyed -> crystal_destroyed.
